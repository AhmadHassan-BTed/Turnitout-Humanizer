# Privacy Policy — Turnitout Core

**Effective Date:** August 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 4, 2026

At **Turnitout Core** ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our academic AI humanizer and citation platform handles your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Manuscript & Document Content
- **Zero Permanent Storage**: All text, documents (`.docx`, `.txt`), citations, and drafts submitted to Turnitout Core are processed **strictly in-memory** during your active browser session.
- We **do not store, log, or index** your submitted manuscripts, papers, or generated outputs on any database or external server.

### B. Authentication & Session Data
- **GitHub Verification**: When you verify your GitHub username, we query GitHub's public REST API to verify repository stargazers and profile follows.
- **Session Cookies**: We use short-lived browser cookies (`turnitout_unlocked`, `turnitout_github_username`) and URL parameters solely to keep you logged in across page reloads. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

- **GitHub REST API**: Used exclusively to check public stargazer and follower status. No private GitHub data is accessed or requested.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not share, sell, or monetize any user data with third parties or telemetry providers.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your manuscript content is deleted immediately upon completion of the transformation request.
- Logging out or clearing browser cookies removes all session data instantly.
