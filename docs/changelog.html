v2.0.0 — Major Architecture & AI Tell Absorption

2026-10-03

✨ Major Additions

  • Master 38-Pattern AI Tell Scrubber: Complete deterministic absorption of the blader/humanizer catalog (§1–§26) plus 12 Turnitout extensions (§27–§38). Eliminates ChatGPT introductory run-ups, meta-commentary, formulaic preambles, and conversational artifacts before main paraphrasing.
  • Zero-Tell Invariant Safety Gate (TellInvariantGate): Strictly enforces the mathematical invariant tells(output) ≤ tells(input) across all three modification retry attempts, rolling back any transformation that adds an AI hallmark.
  • Fact Invariant Gate (FactInvariantGate): Guarantees 100% preservation of numeric data, bracketed citations (e.g. [1], (Author, 2024)), and named entities across sentence modifications.
  • Empirical Voice Matching Engine (Beta): Analyzes user writing samples or uploaded past documents (.docx, .pdf, .tex, .md, .txt) via Turnitout's polymorphic engine to mirror authentic sentence variance, contraction rates, and punctuation rhythm.
  • Same-Subject Clause Coordination Transformer: Automatically detects repeated subject nouns or pronouns across adjacent clauses (§7) and refactors them into natural compound predicates without redundant subject repetition.
  • Document-Level Tell Scrubber: Strips chatbot residue sentences (e.g., "I hope this helps!"), closing summaries, repetitive bold bullet styling, and decorative heading emojis.
  • Load-Time Vocab Hygiene Firewall: Implemented filter_output_vocab() blocking overused AI verbs (delve, foster, bolster) and empty copula avoidance from ever loading as replacement targets, bringing empirical tell additions to 0 across all three presets.
  • Live Transformation Telemetry: Summary dashboard and CLI report now track AI Tells Scrubbed and Subject Coordinations in real time.

⚙️ Pipeline & Preset Refinements

  • Phase-Decoupled Conveyor Belt: Replaced hardcoded array index slices with semantic execution phases ("scrubber", "main", "post", "citation") across all 19 transformers.
  • Appositives Disabled in Academic Preset: Informative parentheticals are now disabled in Academic/Technical mode to preserve dense scholarly register, while active in Casual and Conversational presets.
  • Conceptual Bridges Disabled by Default: Disabled "against this backdrop" and "viewed through this lens" connectors by default to prevent triggering modern AI detector classifiers.
