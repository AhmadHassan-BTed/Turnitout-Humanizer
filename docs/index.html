Does TURNITOUT beat Turnitin AI detection?
TURNITOUT is engineered specifically around the mathematical heuristics used by Turnitin, GPTZero, and CopyLeaks: sliding n-gram perplexity, burstiness variance, and discourse marker density. While no tool can guarantee a perpetual 0% score across every proprietary update, TURNITOUT systematically dismantles the predictable statistical signatures that trigger false alarms.
Why shouldn't I just use QuillBot or another AI paraphraser?
QuillBot uses generative AI to paraphrase text. Turnitin and GPTZero have specifically trained their classifiers on QuillBot outputs, meaning QuillBot-edited drafts are frequently flagged as AI. Furthermore, QuillBot destroys LaTeX equations, strips Word document styling, and caps free users at 125 words.
How does the 100% Executable Code Safety guarantee work?
TURNITOUT uses a character-by-character token scanner that distinguishes between natural language comments (#, //, /* */, docstrings) and runnable syntax. Code logic, functions, loops, variables, and indentation are 100% untouched. Your code runs identically before and after humanization.
