Controlling Word Count: Zero-Expansion Mode

OFFICIAL USER MANUAL

Working on a university essay, journal paper, or dissertation chapter with a strict word limit? If your default transformations added 50 to 100+ words, you are not stuck. You can easily configure Turnitout to perform exact 1-to-1 word replacements with zero net words added.

"By default, natural human writing features cadence variation and discourse connectives. Turnitout's presets include these by default. But when you have a hard word limit, you can toggle them off in seconds."

⚡ The 10-Second Quick Answer

To keep your word count 100% constant (0 added words):

  1. Open Advanced Transformation Settings & Protected Terms.
  2. Keep Synonym Swapping ON and set Synonym Alignment Probability to 0.85 – 0.90.
  3. Turn OFF Transition Injection, Appositive Insertion, and Conceptual Bridging.
  4. Click Transform & Humanize. The engine will perform strict 1:1 substitutions without adding a single extra word.

1. Why Does the Default Preset Add Words?

AI detectors (like Turnitin, GPTZero, and CopyLeaks) evaluate Burstiness—the rhythmic variation in sentence lengths across a paragraph. Uniform sentence lengths are a primary tell of LLMs.

To mimic human drafting cadence, Turnitout's default high-intensity presets inject natural discourse markers (e.g. "In particular", "Consequently"), explanatory appositives (e.g. ", an essential metric,"), and conceptual bridge clauses. Across multiple paragraphs, these helpful stylistic additions naturally expand total word volume by 10% to 20%.

When an assignment has a strict ceiling (e.g., exactly 2,000 words maximum), this expansion needs to be curbed. That is where Zero-Expansion Mode comes in.

2. The Golden Key: 1-to-1 Synonym Swapping & Alignment Ratio

When you run Turnitout with only Synonym Swapping enabled, the engine operates on a strict 1-to-1 replacement model:

1 word in target sentence ➔ 1 context-matched synonym out (Net change = 0 words)

The behavior of this replacement is governed by the Synonym Alignment Probability Slider:

Synonym Alignment Probability 0.50 ➔ 1.00
0.50 (Loose / Casual) 0.70 (Balanced) 0.85 (Standard Sweet Spot) 0.95 (Strict Technical)
  • 0.90 – 0.95 (High Alignment / Strict Academic): Restricts synonym candidates to very close formal equivalents. Eliminates any semantic drift, ideal for hard sciences, legal, engineering, and medical texts.
  • 0.80 – 0.85 (Recommended Default): Provides optimal n-gram chain disruption while ensuring natural reading flow. 100% word-count neutral.
  • 0.70 – 0.75 (Expressive / Casual): Introduces wider vocabulary variety while still keeping word length strictly 1-to-1.
  • Randomize Alignment (Human Variation) Checkbox: When checked, slightly varies the alignment probability per word, creating realistic human stylistic variation without adding any extra tokens.

Here is the definitive guide to every toggle inside Advanced Transformation Settings & Protected Terms:

4. Recommended Profiles for Strict Word Limits

Profile A: "Strict Zero-Expansion" (Exact Word Count Parity)

Target: Exact word match (0 net words added). Ideal for strict word ceilings, grant proposals, and word-capped exams.

  • ON Synonym Swapping (Slider set to 0.85 or 0.90)
  • ON Clause & Word Reorder
  • ON Determiner Swapping
  • ON Information Reordering
  • ON N-Gram Chain Breaking
  • OFF Transition Injection, Appositive Insertion, Conceptual Bridging, Contractions Swapping, Sentence Fusion, Discourse Rotation, Voice Transformations.
Profile B: "Academic Tolerance" (Within ±2% Margin)

Target: For essays with a standard ±10% margin where a minor shift of 15–30 words is completely acceptable.

  • Keep all Profile A settings ON.
  • Enable Nominalization / Verbalization for academic sentence variety.
  • Enable Voice Transformations for varied active/passive dynamics.
  • Leave Appositive Insertion and Conceptual Bridging OFF.

5. Step-by-Step UI Guide: How to Apply in the App

Step 1: Open the Live Application

Launch any of the 8 cluster nodes (e.g., turnitout.streamlit.app) and navigate to the ⚡ Plagiarism & AI Remover page.

Step 2: Expand Advanced Settings

Scroll below the text input area and click on:
:material/tune: Advanced Transformation Settings & Protected Terms

Step 3: Uncheck Expansion Toggles

Uncheck Transition Injection, Appositive Insertion, and Conceptual Bridging. Make sure Synonym Swapping is checked, and position the Synonym Alignment Probability slider at 0.85 or higher.

Step 4: Protect Domain Jargon (Optional)

If your paper contains technical terms, proprietary names, or specific jargon, type them into the Protected Terms box separated by commas. The engine will skip them entirely during synonym replacement.

Step 5: Process and Verify Telemetry

Click Transform & Humanize. Once complete, expand the Telemetry & Transformation Breakdown dropdown to view the exact before and after word count statistics.
